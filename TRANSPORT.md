# 0.50.0: transport atmosphere and sleeper carriage

- Third car of newly created trains is a sleeper; saved third coaches acquire the variant on reload. Three compartments, a side corridor, six lower and six upper berths, twelve sitting places. Right click from the corridor opens/closes the sliding compartment leaf; right click inside sits on a lower berth.
- Shift + right click a berth inside the compartment at night starts rest. Requires a driver and scheduled stations on a closed intercity route. It does not invoke vanilla bed sleep or change respawn points. The player renders lying on the selected upper/lower berth.
- Once all non-spectator players are resting or asleep, ordinary server simulation accelerates until the next stop. The train is not teleported across the map. Morning is applied at arrival and only the sprint started by this feature is stopped. Other players who are awake prevent the skip. Shift, damage, missing driver, leaving or disconnection cancels rest. Saved/abandoned rest seats are released safely on reload.
- Eight individual 27-slot barrels inside each vessel, including upper barrels. Ray-based selection, normal inventory transfers, independent serialization and drop-on-sinking. Existing shared ship cargo is retained for old saves.
- Ship wheel angle is synchronized with rudder input and smoothed on clients. Chair attachment follows cushion height for ship helm, passenger chairs and rail seats, with helm arm pose.
- Six original procedural mono Ogg sounds: steam rhythm, wheel clatter, whistle, braking hiss, hull creak and sea wash. Vehicle loops follow position and tracked speed, fade with motion and distance, and sea noise is quieter inside the hold. Whistle and steam cues accompany station stops/departures. This is the first transport sound pass; settlement conversations and workshop soundscapes remain future work.
- Train teleports now receive the same local vehicle-frame correction as ships. This fixes waking into an adjacent carriage after accelerated travel.

Validation: actual Iris/Emanrux client walking through physical compartment doors, seating, dismounting and walking in a moving sleeper; audible active sound instance; actual night rest, one scheduled station, morning, unchanged respawn and restored simulation; actual Shift cancellation; multiplayer readiness guard with two server player objects (not a full two-client rest test). All eight barrels tested through real UI and item transfers; serialization round trip. Naval cannon/yard integration and rail movement regression also run.
